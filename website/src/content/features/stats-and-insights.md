## The free stats

You don't need Pro to see how you're doing. The Stats screen is free on every plan and covers the numbers most people check:

- **Level and XP**, from the achievements system
- **Completion** over the last 7 days and the last 30 days
- **Check-ins** and **perfect days**
- **Streaks** for every habit, current and best
- **Freeze bank**: how many streak freezes you've saved, up to 3
- **Mood chart** and a line like “You feel best on days you do Reading”
- **Per-habit 30-day completion**
- **Most consistent on {day}**, your strongest weekday

That's enough to answer the everyday questions. Am I doing better than this time last month? Which habit keeps slipping? Is Saturday the problem? Everything is worked out on your phone, so the numbers are there even when you're offline. For a day-by-day view, the [habit calendar](/habit-calendar) shows the same data as a heatmap.

## The Pro Insights section

Insights look for connections between things you already track. With Pro or the 7-day trial, you get four kinds:

| Insight | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| You sleep {time} more after {habit} days | Which habit goes with longer sleep, using your [sleep tracking](/sleep-tracking) data |
| You feel better on days you do {habit} | Which habits line up with better moods |
| {habit} pulls {other} along | Which habit tends to bring another with it |
| Your best time of day | When you're most likely to get habits done |

These are patterns in your own data, not medical findings. Sleep figures are estimates, so treat them as a nudge to experiment rather than a verdict. Plans and prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## How to use your numbers

**Pick one number to watch.** 30-day completion is a good one. It moves slowly, so a bad day doesn't wreck it and a good week shows.

**Fix the weakest habit, not the strongest.** If per-habit completion shows one habit at 40%, make it smaller or move its [reminder](/reminders) before adding anything new.

**Use your best weekday.** If you're most consistent on Tuesdays, start new habits on a Tuesday.

**Let insights suggest experiments.** If walking pulls reading along, try walking first on the days you most want to read.

**Check in weekly, not hourly.** Stats are most useful when you look at them once a week, with the weekly review, and adjust one thing.
