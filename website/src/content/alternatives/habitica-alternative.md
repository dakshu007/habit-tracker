## Who Habitica is great for

Habitica is built for people who are motivated by games. You create a pixel-art avatar, and every real task you tick off earns gold and experience. Miss a Daily and your character loses health. Run out of health and you drop a level and lose your gold.

That loop works well for a lot of people, especially if chores and to-do lists feel dull. Buying a new sword because you flossed every day for a week is a fun reason to floss.

Parties are the other big draw. You can team up with friends (up to 30 per Party), take on quests together and feel the push when your missed task means damage for everyone. Challenges give you ready-made task lists from the community, with Gems for the winner.

Habitica is also everywhere: Android, iOS, the web and a Wear OS watch app, all synced to your account. It's open source, run by a small team with community contributors. If that sounds like you, Habitica is a great pick and you may not need an alternative at all.

## What's different day to day in Habit Tracker

Habit Tracker is for people who like the reward feeling but don't want to manage a game. Here's what a normal day looks like.

**Morning.** You open the app and see a stack of [illustrated habit cards](/habit-cards): water, a walk, ten pages of a book. Slide a card to complete it, feel the haptic tick, and the next one comes up. Finish all of today's habits and you get confetti.

**During the day.** Your phone can do some of the logging. With [Health Connect](/health-connect), steps, workouts, floors and sleep from apps like Samsung Health, Google Fit or Fitbit fill in matching habits. Going for a run before your metro commute? Start the [GPS run tracker](/gps-run-tracker) and the distance lands on your running habit. No watch? [Sleep tracking](/sleep-tracking) can estimate sleep from when your screen stays off inside your bedtime window. These are Pro features and are included in the trial.

**Quick logs.** Five [home screen widgets](/widgets) and a Quick Settings tile let you log a glass of water without opening the app. The Today's habits widget and the tile are free.

**When you slip.** There's no health bar to lose. A missed day only touches your streak, and [streak freezes](/streaks) cover even that: you earn one at every 7-day milestone and can bank up to 3. Rest days pause a streak without breaking it, which helps during exam week or a family wedding. For habits you're quitting, [quit mode](/quit-bad-habits) counts clean days and money saved.

**On Sunday.** A story-style weekly review shows how your week went. The [AI coach](/ai-coach) can look at your last 14 days and suggest one small change. Write in English, Hindi or Hinglish and it replies the same way. Everyone gets one free weekly insight; chat is part of Pro and the trial.

The whole app also works in [Hindi](/hindi-habit-tracker), without an account, and your habits stay on your phone.

## Pricing compared

At the time of writing, Habitica is free to use. An optional subscription and Gem purchases add gear, backgrounds and other extras, and the Google Play listing notes ads and in-app purchases. Group Plans for families or teams cost $9 a month plus $3 per extra member. We couldn't confirm the current individual subscription price on Habitica's own pages, so check the app for your region.

Habit Tracker starts with a 7-day free trial of everything, no card needed. After that you can stay on the free plan forever with 5 active habits, plus streaks, freezes, rest days, reminders, routines, challenges and backup. Pro unlocks unlimited habits, auto-tracking, sleep, runs, the coach chat, four more widgets and monthly reports for ₹99/month, ₹499/year or ₹1,499 once. Outside India it's roughly $3.99, $24.99 or $59.99, and Google Play shows your local price. Full details are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

So if you only track a handful of habits, both apps can cost you nothing. Habitica's paid extras are mostly about the game and groups; ours are about auto-tracking, more habits and the coach.

## How to switch from Habitica

There's no importer, so you'll start fresh. That sounds like a lot, but it takes a few minutes:

1. If you want a record, export your data from the Site Data tab in Habitica's website settings.
2. List the Habits and Dailies you actually want to keep. Leave your To Do's in Habitica or a notes app.
3. In Habit Tracker, add each one from the 21 [habit templates](/habit-templates), like Read, Drink Water, Walk, Workout, Meditate or Journal. Or make a custom habit with any unit and a schedule of every day, chosen weekdays or a number of times per week.
4. Turn Habitica's negative habits into quit habits with the Quit Smoking, No Junk Food or Less Doomscrolling templates.
5. Set reminders, and connect Health Connect if you want steps or workouts to log themselves.

Your old streaks won't carry over. Plenty of people find a clean start motivating anyway.
