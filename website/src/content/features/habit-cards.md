## Why cards instead of a checklist

Most habit apps show a long list of checkboxes. That works, but it feels like a to-do list, and nobody looks forward to a to-do list. Habit Tracker gives each habit its own illustrated card. Your morning run has a runner on it, your evening reading has a book, your water habit has a glass. Swiping through them takes a few seconds and actually feels nice.

There's a practical reason too. One card at a time means one decision at a time. You look at “Meditate, 10 min”, you either do it or move on. No scanning a crowded list and feeling behind before you've started.

It also makes the app easier to come back to after a bad week. A friendly illustration is less of a guilt trip than a red row of unticked boxes, and that matters on the days you're restarting.

## Slide, don't tap

Completing a habit is a slide across the card, not a tap. You feel a short haptic buzz as it locks in. The slide is deliberate on purpose: it's hard to complete something by accident while your thumb is scrolling on a crowded metro.

For habits with a number attached, use the “+” button. Each tap adds the step amount you chose, so a 2 km walk with a 0.5 km step takes four taps, or one slide if you did it all at once. When every card due today is done, confetti drops across the screen.

Completing is final, and past days are view-only. That sounds strict, but it means a 30-day streak really is 30 days. If you forgot to log something yesterday, a [streak freeze](/streaks) may already have covered it.

## Make the stack your own

Pick from 22 illustrations and 16 colour themes for each card. Some people colour-code by time of day (warm colours for morning, cool for night), others by area of life. Four themes are earned through [achievements](/achievements), so a gold card becomes a small trophy you see every day.

If the tilt effect distracts you, turn it off. You can also reorder habits so the one you do first comes first. For more ways to change the look of the app, see [themes and icons](/themes-and-icons).
