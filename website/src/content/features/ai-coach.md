## Why a habit coach beats another reminder

Most habit apps can tell you *that* you missed a day. Very few can tell you *why*, or what to change. That's the gap the AI coach fills. It reads a compact summary of your habits (names, goals, schedules, streaks, the last 14 days of check-ins and your average mood) and answers the question you actually have.

Ask “why do I keep skipping my evening walk?” and you won't get a generic list of motivation quotes. You might hear that you hit your walk on 5 of the last 7 weekdays but almost never on Saturdays, and that a shorter 10-minute weekend version could keep the streak alive. That's the kind of answer a good friend who knows habit science would give.

## What you can ask

The coach is built for everyday habit questions:

- **Getting unstuck:** “I keep breaking my reading streak after 4 days. What should I change?”
- **Planning:** “Plan a better week for me, I have exams from Wednesday.”
- **Starting something new:** “I want to start running but I'm not a morning person.”
- **Quitting:** “How do I stop scrolling in bed?”
- **Sleep and energy:** “I'm tired every afternoon. Which of my habits could help?”

It stays on topic: habits, routines, health and wellbeing habits, focus and productivity, and how to use the app. Off-topic questions get a friendly redirect, and anything medical gets a nudge toward a professional.

## Your weekly insight, free for everyone

Once a week the coach writes one short insight from your data: a pattern you might not have noticed plus one small suggestion for the week ahead. It's free on every plan and saved on your phone for the week, so you can come back to it. Pair it with the [weekly review story](/weekly-review) on Sunday evening for a two-minute reset.

## Private by design

Your chat history is stored only on your phone. When you ask something, the app sends your question, the recent chat and the habit summary to our small server, which passes it to the AI to write the reply. Nothing about what you asked is kept on the server, only a count of replies used. You can report any reply that feels wrong or unhelpful with a long-press. Read more on our [privacy page](/private-habit-tracker).
