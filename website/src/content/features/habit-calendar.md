## Why a heatmap works

A list of check-ins tells you what happened. A heatmap shows you the shape of it. Open the calendar and you can see, in about two seconds, that the first two weeks of the month were solid and the week of your cousin's wedding went pale.

That's useful because habits rarely fail on one bad day. They fade over a run of busy days. The calendar makes those runs obvious, so you can notice the dip while it's still a dip.

## What the calendar shows

The grid shades each day from **Less** to **More** depending on how much you completed. Under it you get three numbers for the month:

- **Goal days:** days you met your goals
- **Perfect days:** days you completed every habit
- **Average completion** across the month

Use the **habit filter** to look at one habit on its own. This is where patterns jump out. Maybe your reading is solid on weekdays but blank every Sunday, or your evening walk disappears whenever you work late. If you're unsure which habits are worth tracking in the first place, our list of [habits to track](/blog/habits-to-track) is a good starting point.

Tap any day to open it. You'll see that day's habits, can complete today's from there (past days stay view-only), or mark the day as a rest day.

A useful end-of-month habit: open the calendar next to your stats. The heatmap shows when things went well, and the numbers show how much.

## Rest days without guilt

Life has fevers, exams, long trips and festival weeks. Instead of watching a streak die, mark the day as a **rest day** from the calendar. It's shaded so you can see it later, your [streaks](/streaks) pause rather than reset, and the day doesn't count against your stats.

A few tips:

- **Plan rest days ahead.** If you know Diwali week will be chaos, mark those days before they arrive.
- **Don't overuse them.** A rest day is for real breaks, not a lazy Tuesday. Streak freezes cover the occasional slip automatically.
- **Look back monthly.** At the end of each month, scan the heatmap and ask one question: what was different about the pale week?

For a longer view, [Year in Review](/year-in-review) puts every day of the year in one heatmap (Pro and trial).
