## Habits are easier with company

Starting a habit alone works, but it's easier when a friend is doing the same thing. You can compare streaks over chai, nudge each other before exams, or both promise to walk 20 minutes after dinner. Invite friends turns that into something useful for you too: free months of Habit Tracker Pro.

The idea is simple. You share a personal link. Your friend gets a longer free trial. When they actually use the app, you get Pro days. Nobody pays anything, nobody needs an account, and there's no card involved at any point.

## How the rewards add up

Every friend who installs from your link and opens Habit Tracker on 3 different days earns you 14 days of Pro. The 3-day rule is there so rewards come from friends who really try the app, not quick installs that get deleted.

| Friends who opened the app on 3 days | Pro days you've earned |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 14 |
| 2 | 28 |
| 3 | 42 |
| 4 | 56 |
| 5 | 90 (includes the 20-day bonus) |

There's one more reward on top: if anyone you invited buys Pro, you get an extra 30 days. All rewards stack, up to 365 reward days per year.

While your reward days last, you have everything in Pro: more than 5 habits, [Health Connect auto-tracking](/health-connect), sleep tracking, GPS runs, voice logging, Insights, all widgets and the AI coach. When the days run out, you're back on the free plan, and your habits and history stay where they are.

## Where to find your link

Open the Profile tab and tap the **Invite friends** card. You'll find your personal link there, with its 6-character code. Copy it into WhatsApp, a family group or any app you like.

A few things worth knowing:

- Self-invites and repeat phones don't count, so you can't earn days by reinstalling on your own phone.
- Your progress is part of your [Google Drive backup](/backup-and-restore), so it survives a phone change.
- Invites use a small server to keep track of codes and rewards. Your habits and logs stay on your phone.

## Who to invite

The best people to invite are the ones who already talk about wanting a routine: the cousin training for a 5K, the flatmate trying to drink more water, the friend who keeps saying they'll read before bed. Start a [habit challenge](/habit-challenges) together with a friend code and you both get a reason to open the app every day.
