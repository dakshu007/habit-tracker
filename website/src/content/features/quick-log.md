## Built for the in-between moments

Most habits get logged in odd moments: waiting for the lift, at a red light in an auto, between two meetings. In those moments, unlocking your phone, finding the app and scrolling to the right card is just enough effort to make you put it off, and then forget. Quick log cuts that down to a swipe and a tap.

It's for anyone who uses their phone in short bursts and wants logging to fit around that. It also helps if you have several habits a day: you can tick off three of them without opening the full app.

## The Quick Settings tile

Android lets apps add tiles to the Quick Settings panel, the same place as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the torch. Habit Tracker adds one called “Log habit”.

Tap it from anywhere, even on top of another app, and a Quick log sheet slides up. It lists today's habits, each with two buttons. Add logs progress toward the goal (a glass of water, 10 minutes, a page). Complete finishes the habit for today. There's a mic in the sheet too, if you'd rather say “drank 2 glasses of water” than tap twice. That uses [voice logging](/voice-logging), which is part of Pro.

## Three shortcuts on the app icon

Long-press the Habit Tracker icon on your home screen and you get three shortcuts:

- **Voice log:** goes straight to voice logging.
- **Quick log:** opens the same Quick log sheet as the tile.
- **Start a run:** opens the [GPS run tracker](/gps-run-tracker), ready to record.

On most Android launchers you can drag a shortcut out onto your home screen as its own icon. A “Start a run” icon next to your music app makes heading out the door that much easier.

## Which fast option to use

You have a few ways to log without opening the app fully, and each suits a different habit:

- **Tile:** best when you're in another app and want to log anything.
- **Widget:** best for habits you check at a glance on your home screen. See [widgets](/widgets).
- **Shortcut:** best for one action you take often, like starting a run.

Plenty of people use all three. The tile and shortcuts are free on every plan.
