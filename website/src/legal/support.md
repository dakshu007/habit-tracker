## Quick fixes

- **Restore a purchase:** Profile → Habit Tracker Pro → Restore purchase (use the same Google account you bought with).
- **Cancel a subscription:** Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Habit Tracker.
- **Move to a new phone:** turn on Profile → Google Drive backup, then choose “Restore from Google Drive” when you set up the app on the new phone. You can also use Profile → Export backup and restore the file.
- **Invite friends:** Profile → Invite friends shows your personal link and how many friends have joined. Friends must install from your link. [How invites work](/invite-friends).
- **AI coach says no replies left:** limits reset daily during the trial and monthly on Pro. See [AI coach](/ai-coach).
- **Reminders not showing:** allow notifications for Habit Tracker and turn off battery optimisation for it in Android settings.
- **Steps or runs not filling in:** Edit habit → Auto-track → allow access in Health Connect. [Health Connect help](/health-connect).
- **Sleep not detected:** Edit your sleep habit → Sleep tracking → Allow usage access. [Sleep tracking help](/sleep-tracking).
- **Add a widget:** long-press your home screen → Widgets → Habit Tracker. [See all widgets](/widgets).
