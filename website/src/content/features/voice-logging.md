## For days when typing feels like too much

You've just finished a run and your hands are sweaty. You're carrying groceries and remember you drank water at the office. You're lying in bed and realise you never logged meditation. In all of these, unlocking the app and finding the right card is a small hassle. Saying one sentence isn't.

Voice logging is for exactly those moments. Say what you did, check what the app understood, tap once. Done.

## How matching works

When you speak, your phone's built-in speech recognizer turns your words into text. Habit Tracker then reads that text, finds the habit it's talking about and the amount, and shows you the match.

Some examples:

- “drank 2 glasses of water” adds 2 glasses to your water habit.
- “ran 5 km” adds 5 km to your running habit.
- “done meditating” completes your meditation habit.
- “meditated 1 hour” counts as 60 minutes for a habit tracked in minutes, since times convert between minutes and hours.

Distances convert too. If your running habit counts kilometres and you say “ran 3 miles”, the app converts it. Metres work the same way, so “walked 800 metres” counts toward a walking habit measured in km.

You always confirm with one tap before anything is saved. If you have two similar habits, like “Walk” and “Evening walk”, say the full name so the match is clear. Short, specific sentences tend to work best.

## Hindi, properly

Habit Tracker treats Hindi as a first-class language, and voice logging is part of that. You can speak in Hindi and use Hindi number words: “दो गिलास पानी” logs 2 glasses of water. With the app set to हिन्दी, the whole experience works in Hindi, from the screens to the [AI coach](/ai-coach). Read more about the [Hindi habit tracker](/hindi-habit-tracker).

## Where to find the mic

There are three ways in. Tap the mic on Home. Open the [Quick log](/quick-log) sheet from the Quick Settings tile and tap its mic. Or long-press the Habit Tracker icon on your home screen and pick “Voice log”.

Because the app never records audio, there's nothing stored to worry about. Your phone's speech recognizer does the listening, and Habit Tracker only uses the resulting text to match your habit.
