## Templates for the habits people actually track

Most people want the same handful of habits: drink more water, read more, move more, sleep better, scroll less. Templates cover those so you don't have to think about the setup. Tap Drink Water and you get a water habit with its illustration ready. Tap Read and you're counting pages.

There are 21 in total. 18 are habits to build, from Running and Walk to Journal, Learn Something and Screen-free Hour. The other 3 are habits to quit, which run in [quit mode](/quit-bad-habits): you confirm a clean day instead of ticking something off, and the app shows days clean and, for smoking and junk food, money saved.

If you're not sure where to start, our list of [habits worth tracking](/blog/habits-to-track) has ideas for different goals.

## Custom habits, built your way

Templates are a starting point, not a box. Every part of a habit can be changed, and you can build one from nothing.

**Goal and unit.** Choose from times, min, hours, km, steps, pages, glasses, laps, floors or reps, or type a custom unit. “Practise guitar, 15 min” and “Revise, 2 chapters” both work.

**Step amount.** This is what each “+” tap adds on the card. A water habit with a step of 1 glass fills up one tap at a time. A reading habit with a step of 5 pages moves faster.

**Schedule.** Every day suits things like water or brushing before bed. Chosen weekdays suit a class you attend on Tuesdays and Thursdays. N times per week suits workouts, where the days shift but the number matters. Weekly habits count their streaks in weeks.

**The personal touches.** Pick one of 22 illustrations and a colour theme. Write a short “why it matters” line (“so I can play with my niece without getting tired”) and a celebration message for when you finish. Add a reminder if you want one.

## Tips for setting goals that stick

Start smaller than feels useful. Two pages of reading done every night beats twenty pages done twice. You can raise the goal once the habit feels automatic.

Match the schedule to your real week. If you know Sundays are family days, leave them out instead of breaking a streak every weekend. And keep your list short: the free plan logs 5 active habits, which is a good number to begin with anyway. Archive the ones that no longer fit; their history stays. To get nudged at the right moment, set up [reminders](/reminders).
