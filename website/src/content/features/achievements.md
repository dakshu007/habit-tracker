## Why points help with boring habits

Drinking water and stretching for five minutes don't feel like much. That's exactly why they're easy to skip: the reward is real but slow. XP gives every one of those small actions an immediate, visible result. The habit is still the point; the number just makes the progress harder to ignore.

The system is simple on purpose. You earn **10 XP** for each habit you complete on a day, and **25 XP** extra when you finish everything due that day. There's nothing to buy: XP only comes from completing habits. If you track 3 habits and finish all of them, that's 55 XP for the day. Do that for a week and you've earned 385.

## Levels and achievements

Your XP moves you through **9 levels**: Seedling, Sprout, Bloom, Grower, Steady, Committed, Relentless, Unstoppable and Legend. The names follow how a habit feels as it grows, from fragile to something you just do. Each new level is a reminder that the small days added up to something. You can see your level and XP at the top of [Stats](/stats-and-insights).

Alongside levels there are **12 achievements** to collect:

- First step, On a roll, Week warrior, Unstoppable
- Perfect day, Perfect week, Fifty strong, Centurion
- Early bird, Night owl, Collector, Rising star

The names give you a hint, and working out what each one takes is part of the fun.

## What you earn

Achievements are also how you unlock four of the 16 habit colour themes: **Ember, Gold, Holo and Midnight**. Three of the 12 profile card gradients are earned the same way. They're cosmetic, but there's something satisfying about a gold habit card you had to work for. See every option on the [themes and icons](/themes-and-icons) page.

## Keep the game in its place

A few ways to make XP work for you rather than the other way round:

- **Don't add habits just for points.** Five habits you do beat ten you skip. Perfect days are worth more when your list is realistic.
- **Use rest days.** A planned rest day keeps your [streak](/streaks) safe and doesn't count against your stats.
- **Look at the trend, not one day.** A level-up every few weeks means you're showing up. That's the real win.
