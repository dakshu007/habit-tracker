## Why sharing helps some people

Saying something out loud changes how it feels. “I'm trying to read more” is easy to drop quietly. A story that says “Day 30 of reading” is harder to abandon, because a few people saw it and one of them will ask about day 31.

Sharing isn't for everyone, and you don't need it to use the app. But if a bit of public commitment keeps you going, Habit Tracker makes it quick and good-looking.

It works the other way too. When a friend posts their streak, it's a reminder that you meant to start something. Sometimes people pick up a habit because someone close to them was visibly doing it, and a card is an easy way to be that person for someone else.

## The cards you can share

**Streak card.** Your streak, front and centre. Good for milestones: your first full week, a month, a hundred days.

**Today card.** Shows how many of today's habits you've finished, like “3 of 4 done”. It's an easy way to tell a friend you're on it, even on a day that isn't perfect.

**Week card.** A summary of your week in one image. It suits a Sunday night post, or a group chat where everyone is working on something.

Every card is 1080×1920, so it fits a story or status without awkward cropping. Nothing is posted for you: the card goes only where you choose to send it.

You can also share two things that aren't cards:

- **Challenge codes.** Start one of the 8 [habit challenges](/habit-challenges), share its code, and your friend starts on the same day.
- **Your weekly review.** At the end of the Sunday [weekly review](/weekly-review), tap Share my week.

## Sharing without oversharing

A few tips to keep it helpful:

- **Share milestones, not every day.** Daily posts get ignored. Day 7, day 30 and day 100 get replies.
- **Pick one or two people who care.** A sibling or a college friend who will actually reply is worth more than a hundred views.
- **Share the honest days too.** “2 of 4 done, long day at work” is more relatable than a run of perfect cards.
- **Make it daily, but private.** If you want someone checking in every day, an [accountability buddy](/accountability-buddy) on WhatsApp works better than a public post.
