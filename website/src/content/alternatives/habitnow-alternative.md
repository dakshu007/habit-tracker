HabitNow and Habit Tracker are both Android apps, both work without making you sign up, and both will happily track a morning walk or a glass of water. The difference is in what each one is trying to be. HabitNow is a planner that does habits, tasks and a calendar together. Habit Tracker does only habits, and spends its effort on making them quicker to log and easier to stick with.

## Who HabitNow is great for

HabitNow suits people who want one app to organise most of their day. You can keep school deadlines, house chores and work tasks next to your habits, sort them into categories and lists, and see everything in a calendar. Goals can be daily, weekly or monthly.

It also has a stopwatch, countdown and interval timers, customisable reminders and alarms, charts for each habit, themes, widgets, an app lock and backups. Premium is a single purchase on Google Play, which many people prefer to a subscription.

If that sounds like exactly what you need, HabitNow is a good choice and you may not need to switch.

## What's different day to day in Habit Tracker

Open Habit Tracker and you see today's habits as a stack of hand-illustrated [habit cards](/habit-cards). Swipe between them and slide a card to complete it. When the last one is done you get confetti and a quick mood check-in. There's no task list or calendar to scroll through first, which is the point: it takes seconds, even on a crowded metro.

A few other things change in daily use:

- **Logging happens in more places.** The free Today's habits widget, a Quick Settings tile in your pull-down shade and app shortcuts let you log without opening the app. See [widgets](/widgets) and [quick log](/quick-log).
- **Some habits fill themselves in.** With Pro or the trial, [Health Connect](/health-connect) can pull in steps, distance, workouts and sleep from your phone, Samsung Health, Google Fit or a watch. [Sleep tracking](/sleep-tracking) can also work from screen-off time in your bedtime window, no watch needed.
- **Streaks are forgiving.** You earn one [streak freeze](/streaks) at every 7-day milestone (bank up to 3), and rest days pause a streak without breaking it.
- **Quitting gets its own mode.** For things like smoking or junk food, [quit mode](/quit-bad-habits) shows days clean and money saved, in ₹ by default.
- **There's someone to ask.** The [AI coach](/ai-coach) knows your habits, streaks and recent completions, and answers in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Chat is in Pro and the trial; everyone gets one free weekly insight.

The whole app is also available in Hindi, including voice logging and shortcuts.

## Pricing compared

At the time of writing, HabitNow is free to download with a one-time Premium upgrade. Google Play lists its in-app items between $0.99 and $11.99 in the US, or ₹80 to ₹798 in India. We couldn't confirm which item is the full Premium unlock, so check the price in the app for your country.

Habit Tracker starts with a 7-day free trial of everything, with no card needed. After that you can stay on the free plan with 5 habits forever, or go Pro for ₹99/month, ₹499/year or ₹1,499 once for lifetime access. Prices elsewhere are roughly $3.99, $24.99 and $59.99, and Google Play shows the final local price. Details are on our [pricing page](/pricing).

So if paying once matters most to you, both apps offer that, though our lifetime plan costs more than HabitNow's in-app items. What you get for it is the auto-tracking, sleep, runs and coach described above.

## How to switch from HabitNow

There's no importer, and we'd rather tell you that up front. The good news is that recreating habits is quick:

1. Install Habit Tracker and finish onboarding. Your 7-day trial starts then.
2. Tap add and pick from the 21 [habit templates](/habit-templates), such as Walk, Read, Drink Water, Meditate or Quit Smoking. Each comes with artwork and a sensible goal.
3. For anything else, make a custom habit with your own goal and unit (minutes, pages, glasses, km and more) and a schedule: every day, chosen weekdays or N times a week.
4. Add reminders, and turn on Google Drive backup so your new history is safe.

Most people are set up in a few minutes. Your streaks start from zero, so it helps to switch on a Monday and treat it as a fresh week. You can keep HabitNow installed for a while if you want to look back at your old charts.
