## Who Habitify is great for

Habitify is a clean, calm habit tracker for people who like data and live across several devices. Your habits sit in a tidy list grouped by morning, afternoon and evening, and real-time sync keeps them the same on your phone, laptop, the web and your watch.

It's also strong on integrations. Habitify can pull in data from Google Fit, Strava, Garmin and Fitbit, and Apple Health on iPhone. It connects to Google and Apple Calendar, and power users get an API plus Zapier and IFTTT. Smart reminders include habit stacking (finish one habit and the next is cued) and location-based nudges.

Add charts and heatmaps, a focus timer, notes, mood tracking and friend challenges with leaderboards, and it's a very complete tool. If you work on a Mac by day and an iPhone by night, Habitify is hard to beat.

## What's different day to day in Habit Tracker

Habit Tracker is Android only, and it uses that focus to make the phone experience feel more personal.

**Checking in.** Instead of a list, you get a stack of [illustrated habit cards](/habit-cards), one per habit. Slide to complete, feel the haptic tick, and move on. Done with everything? Confetti. You can also play habits in order as a [routine](/routines).

**Less typing.** Your phone can log for you. [Health Connect](/health-connect) brings in steps, distance, workouts, floors and sleep from Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit and watches. The built-in [GPS run tracker](/gps-run-tracker) records runs with a live map, pace and per-km splits, and keeps going with the screen off. Without a watch, [sleep tracking](/sleep-tracking) estimates sleep from screen-off time in your bedtime window. These are Pro features and part of the trial.

**From the home screen.** There are five [widgets](/widgets) (habit card, today's habits, mini habit, streak and sleep) and a Quick Settings tile in the pull-down shade. The Today's habits widget and the tile are free.

**When life happens.** [Streak freezes](/streaks) cover a missed day automatically; you earn one every 7-day milestone and can bank 3. Rest days pause a streak during a trip or a fever without breaking it. Both are free. Quit habits show days clean and money saved.

**Getting unstuck.** The [AI coach](/ai-coach) knows your habits and the last 14 days. Ask "Plan a better week for me" and you'll get a short, specific plan in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

And the whole app works in [Hindi](/hindi-habit-tracker), with no account and no ads.

## Pricing compared

At the time of writing, Habitify has a free plan with 3 habits and cross-platform sync. Its pricing page lists Premium at $49.99 a year (about $4.17 a month) or $119.99 for lifetime access. The Google Play listing describes the paid side as two tiers, Plus and Pro, with a 7-day free trial for Pro, so the exact names and prices you see may depend on where and how you buy.

Habit Tracker gives you 7 days of everything with no card. After that, 5 active habits stay free forever. Pro is ₹99/month, ₹499/year or ₹1,499 once, or roughly $3.99, $24.99 or $59.99 outside India. Google Play shows the final local price. More on the [pricing page](/pricing).

Two practical differences matter here. Habitify's free plan includes sync, so 3 habits follow you across every device. Ours stays on one Android phone but gives you 5 habits plus streak freezes, rest days and routines without paying.

## Which one should you choose?

Ask yourself where you check your habits. If the answer is "on my laptop, my iPhone and my watch", Habitify's sync and integrations are the reason to stay. If you like automating things with Zapier or reading your data through an API, the same goes.

If the answer is "on my Android phone, mostly in the morning and before bed", Habit Tracker is built around exactly that moment. You get cards that are quick to slide, widgets and a tile for fast logging, runs and sleep tracked on the phone itself, and a coach that can help when a habit keeps slipping.

## How to switch from Habitify

There's no importer, so you'll recreate your habits. It goes faster than you'd think:

1. Note the habits you track in Habitify, plus their goals and schedules.
2. Add each one in Habit Tracker from the 21 [habit templates](/habit-templates), like Walk, Running, Drink Water, Read, Meditate or Screen-free Hour. Anything else becomes a custom habit with units like minutes, km, steps, pages or glasses.
3. Pick a schedule: every day, chosen weekdays, or a number of times per week.
4. Connect Health Connect so steps and workouts log themselves, and set your reminders.

Most people are set up in a few minutes. Keep Habitify installed for a while if you want to look back at your old charts.
