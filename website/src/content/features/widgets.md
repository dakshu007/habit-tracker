## Why widgets help habits stick

A habit you can see is a habit you remember. That's why advice like “put your running shoes by the door” works. A widget does the same for your phone: every time you unlock it to check messages, your habits are there. Logging one takes a second, and you don't get pulled into the app or anything else.

It also helps on low-motivation days. Seeing “2 of 5 done” on your home screen at 6 PM is often all the push you need to do the third one.

Habit Tracker has five widgets. You can mix them, resize them and place them on any home screen page.

## The five widgets

**Today's habits (4×2, free).** A list of the habits due today. Tap +1 to add one step (a glass of water, 5 minutes of reading) or tap to complete. This is the most useful widget for most people, and it's free on every plan.

**Habit card (3×3).** A large illustrated card for a single habit, like a small version of the [habit cards](/habit-cards) in the app. To complete, tap twice within 20 seconds. Use the ‹ › arrows to switch between habits without leaving the home screen.

**Mini habit (2×2).** One habit, two buttons: +1 and complete. Good for the one habit you care most about right now, like water or a daily walk.

**Streak (2×2).** Your best streak and a view of this week. Handy as a quiet reminder of what you'd lose by skipping, especially with [streak freezes](/streaks) in the bank.

**Sleep (2×2).** Last night's sleep and the week. It pairs with [sleep tracking](/sleep-tracking), whether that's from phone use or Health Connect.

All widgets update every 30 minutes. The Habit card, Mini habit, Streak and Sleep widgets need Pro or the 7-day free trial.

## Layout ideas

- **Minimal:** Today's habits on your first screen, nothing else. It's free and covers daily logging.
- **Focused:** a Mini habit widget for the one habit you're building this month, next to your clock.
- **Motivated:** Habit card plus Streak, so you see the artwork and the number you're protecting together.
- **Sleep-first:** the Sleep widget on your main screen if you're working on going to bed earlier.

Want even faster logging? Add the Quick Settings tile from [quick log](/quick-log) and log from the pull-down shade.
