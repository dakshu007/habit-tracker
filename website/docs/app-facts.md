App: **Habit Tracker: Daily Routine** by Mykavo. Android only (Android 8.0+). Play: `app.mykavo.habittracker`.
Languages: English and Hindi (हिन्दी). No account, no ads.

## Plans, trial, prices
- **Trial:** 7 days of everything, starts when onboarding finishes, no card. One trial per phone (reinstalling does not restart it).
- **Invited friends** get a **14-day** trial instead of 7.
- **Free plan forever (after trial):** 5 active habits fully loggable (the 5 oldest). Other habits stay visible with history but need Pro to log; can't add a 6th active habit.
- **Pro prices** (Google Play shows the final local price): Monthly **₹99/month**, Yearly **₹499/year** (≈₹42/month, "save 58%" vs monthly), Lifetime **₹1,499 once**. Other countries roughly $3.99 / $24.99 / $59.99. Billed by Google Play; cancel anytime in Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.
- **Pro-only:** more than 5 habits, Health Connect auto-tracking, sleep from phone use, starting GPS runs, voice logging, Insights section, Year in Review, monthly PDF/CSV reports, 4 of 5 widgets (Habit card, Mini, Streak, Sleep), AI coach chat.
- **Free forever:** logging/streaks/freezes/rest days for 5 habits; reminders, evening nudge, daily nudges, alarms; Today's habits widget; Quick Settings tile & quick log; focus timer; routines; mood check-ins; calendar/heatmap and basic stats; weekly review story; share cards; WhatsApp buddy message; challenges & friend codes; achievements, levels, themes; file backup & Google Drive backup; 1 AI weekly insight per week; run history viewing.

## Features (slug → facts)
- **ai-coach** — Chat with an AI habit coach (Claude by Anthropic) that sees a short summary of your habits: names, goals, schedules, streaks, last 14 days of completions, average mood. Starter cards: "This week's insight", "Why do I keep skipping?", "Plan a better week for me". Type or speak. Replies short (≈ under 120 words), plain text. Replies in the language you write: English, Hindi or Hinglish. Limits: Pro subscription 300 replies/month, Lifetime 100/month, Trial 10/day, Free 0 chat (but 1 free weekly insight per week for everyone). Weekly insight = one pattern + one small suggestion. Report any reply (Harmful/offensive, Wrong/misleading, Not helpful). Chat history stays on the phone (last 60 messages); server stores only reply counts. Habit/wellbeing/productivity topics only; not medical advice; shares India's Tele-MANAS 14416 if self-harm comes up. 18+ consent on first use.
- **invite-friends** — Personal invite link (6-character code) in Profile. Friend installs from the link → friend gets a 14-day trial. You get **+14 days of Pro** for each friend who opens the app on **3 different days**; **+20 bonus days at the 5th friend** → **5 friends = 90 days = 3 months of Pro free**. Also +30 days when an invited friend buys Pro. Max 365 reward days per year. Rewards stack. Self-invites and repeat phones don't count. Invite progress survives a Google Drive restore.
- **habit-cards** — Each habit is a hand-illustrated card in a swipeable stack. Slide to complete with haptics; confetti when all of today's habits are done. 22 illustrations (anything, cleaning, coding, cycling, dancing, digital detox, gardening, eat healthy, journal, learning, meditate, morning, music, reading, running, self-care, sleep, stairs, swimming, walking, water, workout). 16 colour themes (4 unlocked by achievements). Cards tilt with the phone (can turn off). "+" adds the habit's step amount. Completing is final; past days are view-only.
- **habit-templates** — 21 templates: 18 build (Cycling, Swimming, Dancing, Gardening, Cleaning, Staircase Climbing, Running, Read, Drink Water, Meditate, Walk, Workout, Journal, Sleep Early, Learn Something, Screen-free Hour, Sleep 8 Hours, Eat Greens) + 3 quit (Quit Smoking, No Junk Food, Less Doomscrolling). Or custom: any goal/unit (times, min, hours, km, steps, pages, glasses, laps, floors, reps, custom). Schedules: every day, chosen weekdays, or N times per week. Options: step amount, artwork, colour, celebration message, "why it matters" line, reminder. Reorder/archive.
- **streaks** — Current and best streak per habit (days, or weeks for weekly habits). Earn 1 streak freeze at every new 7-day milestone (7, 14, 21…), bank up to 3; used automatically to cover a missed day. Rest days (from Calendar) pause streaks without breaking them and don't count against stats.
- **quit-bad-habits** — Quit mode: slide to confirm a clean day; shows days clean, best run, slips, money saved (clean days × cost per day; currency setting, default ₹). Template defaults: Quit Smoking ₹150/day, No Junk Food ₹100/day, Less Doomscrolling days only. "I slipped" resets the counter.
- **gps-run-tracker** — Record runs with a live map (MapLibre, OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap): distance, pace, time, pause/resume, hold to finish. Route coloured by pace with km badges. Keeps recording with the screen off (foreground service + "Run in progress" notification); location only "while using the app", no background location. Per-km splits, fastest km, best pace, longest run, weekly/all-time totals. Log to a habit or just track. Routes stored only on the phone. App shortcut "Start a run". Starting runs: Pro/trial; history free.
- **health-connect** — Auto-fill habits from Health Connect (phone step counter, Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, watches). Reads Steps, Distance, Exercise sessions, Floors climbed, Sleep. Sources: steps, any distance, running/walking/cycling distance or time, swimming, yoga, dancing, any workout time, floors, sleep hours. Syncs last 7 days on open; values only go up, so manual logging still works. Pro/trial.
- **sleep-tracking** — Two ways: Health Connect sleep sessions, or from phone use with no watch: time your screen stays off inside your bedtime window counts as sleep (Android 9+, Usage access; only screen on/off times are read, never which apps; nothing leaves the phone). Glances under 2 min don't count as waking. Sleep widget. Pro/trial.
- **voice-logging** — Say "drank 2 glasses of water", "ran 5 km", "done meditating" or Hindi like "दो गिलास पानी": the app matches habit + amount and asks one tap to confirm. Uses the phone's own speech recognizer; the app never records audio. English and Hindi (Hindi number words). Converts min/hours and m/km/miles. Open from Quick log mic, Home, long-press app icon "Voice log". Pro/trial.
- **widgets** — 5 resizable widgets: Habit card (3×3, tap twice within 20 s to complete, ‹ › to switch habits), Today's habits (4×2, +1 and tap to complete — free), Mini habit (2×2, +1/complete), Streak (2×2, best streak + this week), Sleep (2×2, last night + the week). Update every 30 min. 4 are Pro/trial; Today's habits is free.
- **quick-log** — Quick Settings tile "Log habit" in the pull-down shade opens a Quick log sheet (today's habits with Add/Complete + mic). Long-press the app icon for 3 shortcuts: Voice log, Quick log, Start a run. Free.
- **themes-and-icons** — 4 app icons (Sage, Ink, Midnight, Sand), 16 habit colour themes (4 earned), 12 profile-card gradients (3 earned), Light/Dark/System, 7 animation styles (Smooth, Snappy, Gentle, Bouncy, Elastic, Minimal, Off), haptics and tilt toggles, week starts Mon or Sun. Free.
- **focus-timer** — Countdown focus session for a habit: 5, 10, 15, 25 (default), 45 or 60 min; sounds Off, Rain, Ocean, Deep (brown noise), Static (white noise). Screen stays on, ongoing notification, chime at the end, minutes logged to the habit automatically. Free.
- **routines** — Chain habits into a named routine (e.g. Morning routine) and play it one card at a time; slide to complete moves to the next; ends with "x of y habits done". Free.
- **mood-tracking** — After finishing today's habits: "All done for today! How do you feel?" 4 moods (Low, Okay, Good, Great) + optional note. Feeds Stats, insights, weekly review, Year in Review and the coach. Can be turned off. Free.
- **reminders** — Per-habit reminders (every day or the habit's days). Evening nudge (default 8:30 PM, only if habits are still open). Daily nudges: bedtime ("Time to sleep", 10:30 PM), "Good morning plan" (7:30 AM), water breaks every 1/2/3 h (9:00–21:00, stop once water is done). Weekly review notification Sunday 7 PM. Alarms saved to the phone's Clock app (ring on silent; every day/weekdays/once). Free.
- **stats-and-insights** — Free: level & XP, completion over 7/30 days, check-ins, perfect days, streaks, mood chart, "You feel best on days you do X", per-habit 30-day completion, "most consistent on {day}", freeze bank. Pro Insights: "You sleep {time} more after {habit} days", "You feel better on days you do {habit}", "{habit} pulls {other} along", "Your best time of day". All computed on the phone.
- **habit-calendar** — Month heatmap (Less→More), filter by habit, rest days shaded, goal days/perfect days/average completion; tap a day to see or complete habits, mark rest day. Free.
- **weekly-review** — Story-style recap of last week: average completion vs week before, check-ins, best day, longest streak, average mood, "One idea for next week", Share my week. Sunday 7 PM notification. Free.
- **year-in-review** — Active days, perfect days, top and most consistent habit, best month and weekday, moods, distance run and best run, day-by-day year heatmap. Pro/trial.
- **habit-challenges** — 8 challenges: 7-Day Hydration Reset, 7-Day Screen Detox, 21 Days of Reading, 21 Days of Calm, 21-Day Running Start, 30-Day Walking Streak, 30 Days of Journaling, 30-Day Fitness Kickoff. Finish with 80%+ of days done for a trophy. Invite a friend with a code (format like RUN21-261012) to start on the same day. Offline, no accounts, no leaderboards. Free.
- **achievements** — XP: 10 per completed habit-day + 25 per perfect day. 9 levels (Seedling, Sprout, Bloom, Grower, Steady, Committed, Relentless, Unstoppable, Legend). 12 achievements (First step, On a roll, Week warrior, Unstoppable, Perfect day, Perfect week, Fifty strong, Centurion, Early bird, Night owl, Collector, Rising star). Unlock themes (Ember, Gold, Holo, Midnight) and profile cards. Free.
- **monthly-reports** — One-page A4 PDF summary of a month or a CSV of every entry, any of the last 6 months, made on the phone and shared via the share sheet. Pro/trial.
- **share-progress** — 1080×1920 share cards: streak, today ("3 of 4 done"), week. Share challenge codes and the weekly review. Free.
- **accountability-buddy** — Save a buddy's name and optional WhatsApp number; one tap sends today's check-in (done/total, checklist, longest streak, "Keep me honest!"). One-way (no two-way buddy yet). Free.
- **backup-and-restore** — JSON file backup/restore. Google Drive backup to a hidden app-only folder in your own Drive (we never see it); auto backup when you leave the app (at most every 6 h, only if changed), Back up now / Restore, offered at onboarding; restores trial date and invite rewards. Android Auto Backup too. Free.
- **hindi-habit-tracker** — Whole app in English and Hindi (System/English/Hindi). Voice logging, shortcuts and the AI coach also work in Hindi; the coach understands Hinglish. Free.
- **private-habit-tracker** — Works offline, no account, no ads. Habits, logs, runs, sleep and moods stay on the phone. The small server (Supabase, India) holds only an anonymous ID, invite data, a hashed device ID with the trial start, and coach reply counts. AI coach questions are sent to the server and Anthropic to write the reply (not stored, not used for training).

## Do not market
- Founder pricing, reviewer access codes, test builds.
- Two-way buddy, contacts, leaderboards, cloud sync, accounts (they don't exist).
- iPhone/iOS (Android only).
- Don't say widgets complete with one tap for the Habit card widget (it's two taps); "tap to complete" is fine for Today's habits.
- Don't claim "no server at all" or "nothing ever leaves your phone" (coach and invites use a small server).
- No medical claims. Figures from sleep/steps are estimates.
