## Why looks matter for a habit app

You'll open a habit tracker every day, often several times. If it looks dull or feels jarring, that small friction adds up. If it looks the way you like, opening it becomes a small pleasure, and that's half the battle with daily habits.

Habit Tracker gives you control over how it looks and how it moves, and none of it is behind a paywall.

## Icons, colours and dark mode

**App icon.** There are four: Sage, Ink, Midnight and Sand. Change it whenever you change your wallpaper, or match it to the theme you use most.

**Habit colour themes.** There are 16 colour themes for your [habit cards](/habit-cards). Twelve are available from day one. The other four (Ember, Gold, Holo and Midnight) are earned through [achievements](/achievements), so a Holo card on your Home screen means you've put in the work. Many people colour-code: greens for health, blues for learning, warm tones for anything social.

**Profile cards.** Choose from 12 gradients for your profile card. Three are earned through achievements.

**Light, Dark or System.** Dark mode is easier on the eyes when you're logging habits in bed. System follows your phone, so it switches with your phone's schedule.

## Motion, feel and small settings

**Animation styles.** Pick from seven: Smooth, Snappy, Gentle, Bouncy, Elastic, Minimal and Off. Snappy suits people who want things fast. Bouncy and Elastic are more playful. Minimal and Off are there if motion bothers you or you want the app to feel as quick as possible.

**Haptics.** Sliding a habit to complete gives a small vibration. If you'd rather not feel it, switch it off.

**Tilt.** Habit cards move slightly as you tilt the phone. It's a nice touch, but you can turn it off if you prefer cards to stay still.

**Week start.** Set your week to begin on Monday or Sunday. Pick the one that matches how you think about your week, especially if you track habits a few times per week.

None of these settings change your data. Switching theme, icon or animation style is instant and you can change your mind as often as you like, so it's worth trying a few combinations in your first week.
