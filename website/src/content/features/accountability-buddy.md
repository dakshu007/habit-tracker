## Why one person beats a hundred followers

Accountability works best when it's personal. A public post gets a few likes and disappears. A message to one friend who knows you're trying to wake up at 6 gets a reply: “Did you actually go for the run?”

That small social nudge is what an accountability buddy is for. You pick one person, and every day you tell them how it went. It's harder to skip your evening walk when you know you'll have to send “1 of 3 done” to your best friend.

If you're still working out which habits to start with, our guide on [how to build good habits](/blog/how-to-build-good-habits) is a good place to begin.

## What gets sent

When you tap to check in, Habit Tracker sends a WhatsApp message built from today's data:

- **Done vs total**, like “3 of 4”
- **Your checklist**, so your buddy can see what's done and what's still open
- **Your longest streak**
- **“Keep me honest!”**, a light prompt for them to reply

You don't type anything, which matters. On a tired day, writing an update is just another chore. One tap is easy enough to do even when you're behind.

## What it doesn't do (yet)

We want to be clear about this. The buddy feature is **one-way**. You send check-ins; your buddy reads them in WhatsApp and replies there. They don't get an app view of your habits, they can't send check-ins back through Habit Tracker, and there's no shared dashboard or leaderboard.

If you both want to track habits, each of you can use the app and send your own check-ins to each other. To get a friend started, send them your [invite link](/invite-friends): they get a 14-day trial instead of 7.

## Making it work

**Choose someone who will reply.** The best buddy isn't the most disciplined person you know. It's the one who will actually text back.

**Agree on a time.** “I'll send it before 10 PM” turns a nice idea into a routine.

**Send it on bad days too.** “1 of 4, rough day” keeps the habit of checking in alive, and that's the habit that rescues the others.

**Do a challenge together.** Start the same [habit challenge](/habit-challenges) with a friend code and send check-ins to each other while you do it. For bigger milestones, a [share card](/share-progress) of your streak makes a nice change from the daily message.
