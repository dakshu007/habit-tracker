## For people who already track their health

If you wear a watch, use Samsung Health or Google Fit, or simply carry your phone everywhere, you're already collecting data about your day. Typing the same numbers into a habit tracker is the kind of small job that makes people quit after a week. Health Connect sync removes it.

Health Connect is Android's shared space for health data. Apps write to it, other apps read from it with your permission. Habit Tracker reads Steps, Distance, Exercise sessions, Floors climbed and Sleep, then fills the habits you've linked.

## What you can auto-track

You choose what fills each linked habit. The sources cover most activity habits:

- **Steps** for a daily walking goal.
- **Any distance**, or **running, walking or cycling** distance or time on its own.
- **Swimming**, **yoga** and **dancing** sessions.
- **Any workout time**, for a general “move 30 minutes” habit.
- **Floors climbed**, for a stairs habit.
- **Sleep hours**, for a habit like Sleep 8 Hours.

The [habit templates](/habit-templates) for Walk, Running, Cycling, Swimming, Staircase Climbing and Sleep 8 Hours pair naturally with these sources. Want to record a run with a map instead? Use the built-in [GPS run tracker](/gps-run-tracker).

A simple example: set a Walk habit to 8,000 steps with steps as its source. Walk to the station, around the office and to the shop in the evening, and your card fills up through the day without you touching it. Open the app at night and it's already complete.

## How syncing behaves

Habit Tracker syncs when you open it, looking back over the last 7 days. If you were travelling and didn't open the app for three days, those days still fill in.

Values only go up. That rule makes manual logging and sync work together: if your watch missed a walk because it was charging, add it by hand and the next sync won't lower the number. Completed habits stay completed.

## Good to know

Step counts, distances and sleep from phones and watches are estimates, and different devices can disagree. Treat them as a useful guide for building a habit, not a medical measurement. If you have a health condition, talk to your doctor about the right activity goals for you.

Your synced values are stored on your phone with the rest of your habits. For sleep without a watch, see [sleep tracking from phone use](/sleep-tracking).
