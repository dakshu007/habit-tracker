## For runners who want one app, not two

Plenty of people start running as a habit, then end up juggling a running app for the route and a habit app for the streak. Habit Tracker puts both in one place. Record the run, and the distance goes to your running habit. Your streak, your stats and your [Year in Review](/year-in-review) all know you ran.

It's built for everyday runners: the 5 km before work, the slow evening jog in the park, the [21-Day Running Start challenge](/habit-challenges). No account, no feed, no social pressure.

## What a run looks like

Tap Start and the map follows you. Distance, pace and time update as you go. Stop at a crossing, tap pause, then resume. When you're done, press and hold to finish. The hold is there so a jacket pocket can't end a 10 km run at 6.

You can lock the screen the whole time. Android shows a “Run in progress” notification while recording, which is how the app keeps tracking without background location permission. Location is used only while the app is in use.

Afterwards you get your route coloured by pace, with a badge at each kilometre. Your per-km splits show whether you started too fast. Records keep your fastest km, best pace and longest run, and the totals add up your week and all time.

## Maps and privacy

Maps come from OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap, drawn with MapLibre, so there's no third-party fitness account to sign up for. Your routes are stored only on your phone, which matters when most of your runs start at your front door.

## Tips for building a running habit

- Make the habit about showing up first. A goal of “run 10 minutes” on chosen weekdays is easier to keep than “run 5 km daily”.
- Use the “Start a run” shortcut from your home screen so starting takes one long-press.
- Look at your splits once a week, not after every run. If your first kilometre is always your fastest, start slower and see if the last one improves.
- Use your weekly total as the number to grow, not your pace. Adding one more short run a week is easier to keep than chasing a faster 5 km.
- If you also wear a watch, you can count runs from it through [Health Connect](/health-connect) instead.
