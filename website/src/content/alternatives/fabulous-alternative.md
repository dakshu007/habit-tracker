## Who Fabulous is great for

Fabulous describes itself as a habit tracker that grew into a self-improvement, coaching and mental health platform, and that's a fair summary. It's built for people who want guidance, not just a checklist.

When you start, you pick a Journey and Fabulous gives you small daily steps toward it. Along the way there are coaching series grouped by topics like anxiety, productivity and self-love, plus guided meditation, breathing exercises, short workouts, affirmations and a gratitude journal. Premium adds one-on-one sessions with a real coach.

It also has live challenges where you go through a theme alongside other members, and it runs on Android, iPhone and Apple Watch. Fabulous began at Duke University's Center for Advanced Hindsight and has won design recognition, including a Google Material Design Award.

If you want an app that teaches and nudges you through a routine, Fabulous is a strong choice.

## What's different day to day in Habit Tracker

Habit Tracker is for people who already know roughly what they want to do and need help doing it consistently. There's less to read and more to tap.

**Morning.** You open the app to a stack of [illustrated habit cards](/habit-cards). Slide to complete, feel the haptic tick, and the next card slides up. If you've set up a [routine](/routines), you can play it from start to finish: water, stretch, make chai, ten minutes of reading.

**Through the day.** Your phone handles some of the logging. With [Health Connect](/health-connect), steps and workouts from Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit or your watch fill in matching habits. The built-in [GPS run tracker](/gps-run-tracker) records your evening run with a live map. Without a watch, [sleep tracking](/sleep-tracking) estimates sleep from when your screen stays off in your bedtime window. These are Pro features, included in the trial.

**Quick check-ins.** A Quick Settings tile and five [widgets](/widgets) let you log a habit from the home screen or the pull-down shade. The Today's habits widget and the tile are free.

**When things go wrong.** Missed a day? A streak freeze covers it automatically, and rest days pause a streak without breaking it. For habits you're cutting down, quit mode counts clean days and shows money saved.

**When you want advice.** The [AI coach](/ai-coach) knows your habits, streaks and last 14 days. Ask "Why do I keep skipping?" and you'll get a short, specific answer. Write in English, Hindi or Hinglish and it replies the same way. It sticks to habits, wellbeing and productivity, and it isn't medical advice.

The whole app works in [Hindi](/hindi-habit-tracker), with no account and no ads.

## Pricing compared

At the time of writing, Fabulous is a free download with a Premium plan billed monthly or annually. Its trial converts to a paid subscription unless you cancel before it ends, as its own FAQ explains. The US App Store lists several Premium options between $16.99 and $59.99, and prices vary by country and offer, so check the app for yours. The free version is limited: unlimited habits for your routines are part of Premium.

Habit Tracker gives you 7 days of everything with no card, so there's nothing to cancel. After that, 5 active habits stay free forever, with streaks, freezes, reminders, routines, the focus timer and mood check-ins. Pro is ₹99/month, ₹499/year or ₹1,499 once (roughly $3.99, $24.99 or $59.99 elsewhere). See the [pricing page](/pricing) for what's in each plan.

## Which one should you choose?

It mostly comes down to what you want from the app each morning.

If you want to be guided, with a coach's voice, a Journey to follow and a meditation at the end, Fabulous is built for that, and the coaching library is something we don't try to copy.

If you want to open your phone, tick off five habits in under a minute and get on with your day, Habit Tracker fits better. It's also the better pick if you want steps, runs or sleep logged without typing, or a coach you can talk to in Hindi or Hinglish.

## How to switch from Fabulous

There's no importer, so you'll set things up fresh. It's quicker than it sounds:

1. Write down the habits in your current Fabulous routines.
2. In Habit Tracker, add each from the 21 [habit templates](/habit-templates): Drink Water, Meditate, Workout, Journal, Read, Sleep Early, Walk and more. Anything else becomes a custom habit with your own unit and schedule.
3. Group your morning habits into a routine so you can play them in order.
4. Add reminders, and connect Health Connect if you want movement or sleep to log itself.

Most people are done in a few minutes and logging the same day.
