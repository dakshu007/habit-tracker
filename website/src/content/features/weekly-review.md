## Why a weekly look back works

Daily tracking is good at keeping you moving. It's bad at showing direction. On any given Wednesday it's hard to tell whether you're doing better than a month ago or just getting by.

A week is the right size for that question. It's long enough to smooth out one bad day and short enough that you still remember why Thursday fell apart. The weekly review gives you that check-in automatically, every Sunday evening, in about two minutes.

It also changes how a bad day feels. Missing Wednesday's workout stings less when you know you'll look at the whole week on Sunday and can see that four out of five other days went fine.

## What's in the story

The review plays like a short story you tap through:

1. **Average completion** compared with the week before
2. **Check-ins**: how many habits you completed in total
3. **Best day**: the day everything clicked
4. **Longest streak** of the week
5. **Average mood**, if you log [mood check-ins](/mood-tracking)
6. **One idea for next week**
7. **Share my week**, if you want to send it on

Each card shows one thing, so you can get through it on the sofa with your evening chai. There's no wall of charts to dig through. If you want more detail afterwards, the Stats screen and the calendar have the full picture.

## A simple Sunday routine

Here's a five-minute routine that pairs well with the review:

- **Open the review when the 7 PM notification arrives.** Sunday evening is natural planning time, after the weekend and before Monday.
- **Look at one number.** Completion up or down? That's the headline. Don't overthink it.
- **Ask why your best day worked.** Did you wake up earlier? Skip the late-night scrolling? Repeat that on purpose.
- **Pick the idea or make your own.** Write down when you'll do it, like “Tuesday, walk after lunch instead of after work”.
- **Read your AI weekly insight too.** Every plan gets one free [AI weekly insight](/ai-coach) a week with a pattern from your data.

If the week went badly, don't skip the review. That's the week it's most useful. Look for the one day that did work and build from there.

If you're proud of the week, tap Share my week and send it to whoever keeps you honest. The [share cards](/share-progress) page shows the other things you can post.
