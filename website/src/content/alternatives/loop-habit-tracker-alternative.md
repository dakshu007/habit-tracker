## Who Loop is great for

Loop is a free, open-source habit tracker for Android, and it's genuinely good. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no limit on how many habits you track. It works offline, needs no account, and its Play listing says it collects no data. The code is public under GPLv3, and you can install it from Google Play or F-Droid.

Its standout idea is the habit score. Every repetition makes a habit stronger and every miss makes it weaker, but a few missed days after a long run don't wipe out your progress. Add detailed charts, flexible schedules like "3 times a week" or "every other day", colourful widgets, reminders you can tick from the notification, and CSV or SQLite export, and you have a tracker many people use happily for years.

If you want something free, private and focused, and you're happy logging everything yourself, Loop is a great choice. We mean that.

## What's different day to day in Habit Tracker

Habit Tracker is for people who want their phone to do more of the work, and who'd like tracking to feel a bit warmer.

**Checking in.** You see a stack of [illustrated habit cards](/habit-cards), with 22 illustrations from reading to cycling. Slide to complete, feel the haptic tick, and the next card comes up. You can also chain habits into a [routine](/routines) and play it one card at a time.

**Auto-tracking.** This is the biggest difference. [Health Connect](/health-connect) fills in steps, distance, workouts, floors and sleep from Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit or a watch. The built-in [GPS run tracker](/gps-run-tracker) records runs with a live map, pace and per-km splits. No watch? [Sleep tracking](/sleep-tracking) estimates sleep from screen-off time in your bedtime window. All of this is Pro, included in the trial.

**Logging fast.** Five widget types (habit card, today's habits, mini habit, streak and sleep), a Quick Settings tile in the pull-down shade, and voice logging: say "ran 5 km" or "दो गिलास पानी" and confirm with one tap. See [widgets](/widgets) and [quick log](/quick-log).

**Missed days.** Where Loop softens misses with its score, we use [streak freezes](/streaks) (earned every 7-day milestone, up to 3 banked) and rest days that pause a streak without breaking it. Quit habits show days clean and money saved.

**Getting advice.** The [AI coach](/ai-coach) knows your habits and last 14 days and replies in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Everyone gets one free weekly insight; chat is Pro.

## Pricing compared

This part is simple: Loop is free, and Habit Tracker isn't entirely.

Habit Tracker gives you 7 days of everything with no card. After that, 5 active habits stay free forever, with streaks, freezes, rest days, reminders, routines, the focus timer, challenges, the Today's habits widget and backup. Pro unlocks unlimited habits and the features above for ₹99/month, ₹499/year or ₹1,499 once (roughly $3.99, $24.99 or $59.99 outside India). See the [pricing page](/pricing).

So the honest question is whether auto-tracking, runs, sleep, the coach and the extra polish are worth that to you. If not, stay with Loop.

## Which one should you choose?

Think about what's stopping you today. If the problem is simply remembering to log, and you like clean charts, Loop already solves it for free. There's no reason to pay for something you won't use.

If the problem is that logging feels like a chore, that you forget to enter your walks and runs, or that you want someone to talk through a bad week with, that's where Habit Tracker earns its place. The free plan lets you try the core with 5 habits, and the 7-day trial shows you every Pro feature first.

Some people run both for a week: Loop for the habits they already track, Habit Tracker for runs and sleep. That's a fair way to decide.

## How to switch from Loop

There's no importer, so you'll recreate your habits. It takes a few minutes:

1. In Loop, export your data to CSV or SQLite so you keep your full history.
2. List the habits you want to carry over, with their schedules.
3. In Habit Tracker, add each from the 21 [habit templates](/habit-templates), like Read, Walk, Running, Drink Water, Meditate or Sleep 8 Hours, or make a custom habit with any unit.
4. Set schedules (every day, chosen weekdays or a number of times per week) and reminders.
5. Connect Health Connect if you want steps or workouts to log themselves.

Your streaks start fresh. You can keep Loop installed alongside while you decide.
