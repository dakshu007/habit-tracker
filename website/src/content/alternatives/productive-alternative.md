Productive has been helping people build routines for years, and it's popular on the App Store. It's also on Google Play, which is why many Android users find it first. If you're comparing it with Habit Tracker, the short version is this: Productive is a cross-platform app with a community angle, and Habit Tracker is an Android-only app focused on quick logging, auto-tracking and coaching.

## Who Productive is great for

Productive works well if you like structure across your day. Habits are grouped into morning, afternoon and evening, and smart reminders tell you what's scheduled for each part of the day. You can give each habit its own icon and colour, pause habits when life gets busy, and follow your chains of completed days in the stats.

On the App Store listing, Premium adds unlimited habits, challenges you complete with other users, guided programs with daily tips, full articles, location-based reminders and week-by-week habit trends. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch as well as Android, which matters if you, or your family, use both.

One honest tip: the Google Play description lists fewer Premium features than the App Store one, so if you're on Android, check that the feature you care about is there before you subscribe.

## What's different day to day in Habit Tracker

In Habit Tracker, each habit is a hand-illustrated [habit card](/habit-cards). You swipe through today's stack and slide a card to complete it, with haptics and confetti when you finish the day. It's quick enough to do while your chai is brewing.

Here's what else feels different:

- **Logging without opening the app.** The free Today's habits widget, a Quick Settings tile and app shortcuts let you tick things off from anywhere. See [widgets](/widgets) and [quick log](/quick-log).
- **Auto-tracking.** With Pro or the trial, [Health Connect](/health-connect) can fill in steps, distance, workouts and sleep from your phone, Samsung Health, Google Fit or a watch.
- **Sleep without a watch.** [Sleep tracking](/sleep-tracking) can estimate sleep from how long your screen stays off in your bedtime window (Pro/trial).
- **Forgiving streaks.** You earn a [streak freeze](/streaks) at every 7-day milestone and can mark rest days, so one bad day doesn't reset weeks of effort.
- **Quit mode.** For habits you're breaking, [quit mode](/quit-bad-habits) tracks days clean and money saved.
- **An AI coach.** The [AI coach](/ai-coach) sees a summary of your habits and streaks and replies in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Chat is in Pro and the trial; everyone gets one free weekly insight.

The app itself is fully available in Hindi, and there are no ads on any plan.

## Pricing compared

At the time of writing, Productive is free to download with limited habits, and Premium is a subscription with a 7-day free trial. The US App Store lists Premium at $10.99 a month, and shows several yearly plans priced from $19.99 to $79.99, so the price you see may depend on the offer and your country.

Habit Tracker also starts with a 7-day trial of everything, and you don't need a card. After that you can keep 5 habits free forever, or choose Pro at ₹99/month, ₹499/year, or ₹1,499 once for lifetime access. Outside India it's roughly $3.99, $24.99 and $59.99, and Google Play shows your local price. See [pricing](/pricing) for what's free and what's Pro.

If you'd rather not subscribe at all, our lifetime plan is a one-time payment.

## How to switch from Productive

There's no importer, so your Productive history stays where it is. Setting up again is quick:

1. Install Habit Tracker and finish onboarding. The 7-day trial starts there.
2. Add habits from the 21 [habit templates](/habit-templates), like Walk, Read, Drink Water, Meditate, Workout or Less Doomscrolling.
3. For anything else, create a custom habit with any goal and unit, scheduled every day, on chosen weekdays or N times a week.
4. If you liked Productive's morning and evening structure, chain habits into [routines](/routines) and play them one card at a time.
5. Turn on Google Drive backup so your new streaks are safe.

Give it a few minutes and you'll have your routine back, starting fresh from day one. If you're subscribed to Productive Premium, remember to cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions when you're ready.
