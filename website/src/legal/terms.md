## Using the app

Habit Tracker is a personal habit and wellbeing app made by Mykavo. By installing or using it you agree to these terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the app.

## Free trial, free plan and Habit Tracker Pro

Everyone can use every feature free for 7 days from setup, no payment needed (14 days if you joined through a friend’s invite). After the trial you can keep logging 5 habits for free, forever. Logging more habits and using Pro features requires Habit Tracker Pro. Your existing history always stays viewable.

Habit Tracker Pro is available as:

- **Monthly:** ₹99 per month, renews automatically every month.
- **Yearly:** ₹499 per year, renews automatically every year.
- **Lifetime:** ₹1,499, a one-time payment.

Prices are shown in your Google Play account’s currency and may differ by country. Taxes are included where Google Play requires it.

## Billing and cancellation

All payments are processed by Google Play under Google’s terms. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel anytime in the Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled according to Google Play’s refund policy.

## Invite rewards

You can earn Pro time by inviting friends: 14 days for each invited friend who uses the app on 3 different days, a 20-day bonus at your 5th qualifying friend, and 30 days when an invited friend buys Pro, up to 365 reward days per year. Self-invites and repeat installs on the same phone don’t count.
