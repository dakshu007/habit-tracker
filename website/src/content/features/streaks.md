## Why streaks work, and where they go wrong

A streak is a simple promise to yourself: don't break the chain. Seeing “23 days” on your meditation card makes it a little harder to skip day 24. That small pressure is useful.

The problem is what happens after a miss. You go to a wedding, get a fever, or work late, and a 40-day streak drops to zero. Plenty of people stop the habit right there, because starting again from 1 feels pointless. Habit Tracker is built so that one off day doesn't wipe out weeks of effort.

Remember too that habits take time. In a well-known study by Lally and colleagues (2009), people took a median of 66 days for a new behaviour to feel automatic, with a wide range of 18 to 254 days. A streak that survives a few rough days helps you get there. More on that in [how long it takes to form a habit](/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-form-a-habit).

## How freezes work

Every time a streak reaches a new 7-day milestone (7, 14, 21, 28 and onward) you earn one streak freeze. You can bank up to 3.

When you miss a scheduled day, a freeze is used automatically. You don't need to open the app or tap anything. The next morning your streak is still there and your bank has one fewer freeze. You can see your freeze bank in Stats.

Because freezes come from consistency, they reward the habits you've already proven you can keep. Do a week, earn a safety net. Do three weeks, and you have room for a long weekend away.

## Rest days for planned breaks

Some breaks you see coming: a trip home for Diwali, a week of board exams, a planned recovery day after a long run. For those, use rest days. Open the [habit calendar](/habit-calendar), tap the day and mark it as rest. That day pauses your streaks without breaking them, and it isn't counted against your completion stats.

Use rest days honestly. They're for real breaks, not for the evening you didn't feel like it.

## Make streaks part of something bigger

Consistency also feeds your XP and [achievements](/achievements): every completed habit-day earns 10 XP, and each perfect day adds 25 more as you climb from Seedling toward Legend. Weekly habits get their own streaks counted in weeks, so a 3-times-a-week gym habit is judged fairly.
