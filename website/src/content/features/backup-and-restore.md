## Why back up a habit tracker

A 200-day streak is a small thing to lose until you lose it. Phones get dropped in the metro, stolen at a concert, or wiped during an update. Habit Tracker keeps your data on your phone, which is good for privacy. It also means a backup is worth setting up on day one.

The good news: it takes a minute, it's free on every plan, and after that you can forget about it. There's no account to create, because Drive backup uses your existing Google account.

## Two ways to back up

**Google Drive backup.** The easiest option. Your backup goes into a hidden, app-only folder in your own Google Drive. It doesn't show up among your normal files, it isn't stored on our server, and we never see it. Once it's on, the app backs up automatically when you leave it, but only if something changed, and at most once every 6 hours. Tap **Back up now** whenever you want a fresh copy, and **Restore** to bring it back.

You'll be offered Drive backup during onboarding. If you skipped it, you can turn it on later in the app.

**File backup.** If you'd rather not use Google Drive, save a JSON backup file. Keep it on your computer, a pen drive or anywhere you trust, and restore from it whenever you need. It's also handy for keeping a copy before you try something new.

On top of both, the app supports **Android Auto Backup**, Android's own backup system, as an extra safety net.

## Moving to a new phone

1. On your old phone, tap **Back up now** to make sure the latest day is saved.
2. Install Habit Tracker on the new phone.
3. Restore from Google Drive with the same Google account, or restore from your backup file.
4. Check your streaks and you're done.

A Google Drive restore also brings back your **trial date and invite rewards**. So if you earned extra Pro days by [inviting friends](/invite-friends), they come with you, and your invite progress carries on.

A backup is different from an export. If you want a readable summary or a spreadsheet of your entries, use [monthly reports](/monthly-reports) instead. To see exactly what Habit Tracker stores and where, read the [privacy page](/private-habit-tracker).
