## Why check in after your habits

Most mood trackers ask you at a random time of day, and the answer depends on whether your metro was late. Habit Tracker asks at a more useful moment: right after you've finished today's habits. That ties each mood to a specific day of routines, which makes the patterns easier to read later.

It also means the question comes at a moment of small success. You've just finished your list, so the check-in feels like closing the day rather than a chore.

It's quick. Four choices, Low, Okay, Good or Great, plus an optional note. You can add “exam tomorrow” or “walked with Ammi in the park” if you want context, or just tap and close.

## Where your moods show up

Your check-ins feed several parts of the app:

- **Stats:** a mood chart over time, plus a line like “You feel best on days you do Reading”. Both are free.
- **Weekly review:** your average mood for the week sits next to your completion and best day in the Sunday [weekly review](/weekly-review).
- **Pro Insights:** a deeper look at which habits line up with feeling better. This lives in the Insights section of [Stats and insights](/stats-and-insights), part of Pro and the trial.
- **Year in Review:** your moods across the whole year (Pro and trial).
- **AI coach:** when you chat, the coach sees your average mood with your habit summary (Pro and trial).

All the number crunching happens on your phone.

## Getting useful patterns

**Be honest, especially on Low days.** A chart full of Greats teaches you nothing. A Low on a day you still showed up is worth recording, because it shows which habits you can rely on when things are hard.

**Give it time.** One week is a mood. A month starts to look like a pattern.

**Act on one thing.** If walking shows up on your best days, protect it. Put it before the thing that usually crowds it out, or give it a [reminder](/reminders) at a time you're likely to say yes.

**Notes help later.** “Slept 5 hours” next to a Low explains a lot more than the mood alone.

**Compare weeks, not days.** When the Sunday review shows your average mood, ask what was different about your best week. Often it's something simple, like more sleep or fewer late dinners.
