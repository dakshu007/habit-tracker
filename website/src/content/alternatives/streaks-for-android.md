Streaks is one of the best-known habit trackers on the iPhone. It's simple: pick up to 24 tasks, do them every day and watch the streaks grow. It can also tick tasks off for you using the Apple Health app, and it has a proper Apple Watch app. The catch, if you're reading this, is that it's only on Apple devices. There's no Streaks for Android.

So this page is for people who moved to Android, or who heard about Streaks and want something like it on their phone. Here's how Habit Tracker compares.

## Who Streaks is great for

Streaks is a great fit if you live in Apple's world. It syncs your tasks across devices through iCloud, works on your wrist, and reads Health data with your permission, so a step goal or workout can complete itself. You can create negative tasks for habits you're breaking and timed tasks for things like meditation. You can also share tasks with other Streaks users.

It's sold as a one-time purchase on the App Store, so there's no subscription. If you have an iPhone and an Apple Watch, it's hard to argue with.

## What's different day to day in Habit Tracker

Habit Tracker keeps the same core idea: a small set of habits, done daily, with your streak front and centre. Each habit is a hand-illustrated [habit card](/habit-cards). You slide to complete it, feel a little haptic buzz, and get confetti when the day is done.

Here's how the things Streaks is known for translate:

- **Apple Health becomes Health Connect.** With Pro or the trial, [Health Connect](/health-connect) fills in steps, distance, workouts, floors and sleep from your phone, Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit or a watch. Values only go up, so manual logging still works.
- **Negative tasks become quit mode.** [Quit mode](/quit-bad-habits) shows days clean, your best run and money saved, in ₹ by default.
- **Timed tasks become the focus timer.** The free [focus timer](/focus-timer) runs 5 to 60 minute sessions with optional rain or ocean sounds, and logs the minutes to your habit.
- **Watch complications become widgets.** You get 5 home screen [widgets](/widgets) and a Quick Settings tile for logging from the pull-down shade. The Today's habits widget and the tile are free.

And a few things Streaks doesn't offer, as far as we could find:

- **[Streak freezes](/streaks).** Earn one at every 7-day milestone, bank up to 3, and they cover a missed day automatically. Rest days pause a streak without breaking it.
- **[Sleep tracking](/sleep-tracking) without a watch.** It can estimate sleep from screen-off time in your bedtime window (Pro/trial).
- **An [AI coach](/ai-coach).** It sees a summary of your habits and replies in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Chat is in Pro and the trial; everyone gets a free weekly insight.

The trade-offs are real: no watch app, and no cloud sync between devices. Your data stays on your phone, with Google Drive backup if you turn it on.

## Pricing compared

Streaks is a one-time purchase on the App Store at the time of writing. The price varies by country, so check your local store.

Habit Tracker gives you a 7-day free trial of everything, no card needed. After that, you can keep 5 habits free forever, or go Pro for ₹99/month, ₹499/year or ₹1,499 once for lifetime access. Outside India it's roughly $3.99, $24.99 and $59.99, with Google Play showing your final local price. If you liked paying once for Streaks, the lifetime plan works the same way. Full details are on our [pricing page](/pricing).

## How to switch from Streaks

There's no way to import Streaks data, and it doesn't come with you from iPhone to Android anyway. Starting fresh is quick:

1. Install Habit Tracker and finish onboarding. Your trial starts there.
2. Pick from the 21 [habit templates](/habit-templates), like Walk, Read, Meditate, Drink Water, Sleep 8 Hours or Quit Smoking.
3. Recreate anything else as a custom habit, with your own goal and unit and a schedule of every day, chosen weekdays or N times a week.
4. If some of your Streaks tasks used Apple Health, connect Health Connect and link those habits to steps, workouts or sleep.
5. Turn on Google Drive backup so your new streaks are safe if you change phones.

Your old 200-day streak won't carry over, which stings a bit. But your habits are still yours, and most people have everything set up again in a few minutes.
