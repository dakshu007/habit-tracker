## Who a Hindi habit tracker is for

Plenty of habit apps only work in English. Habit Tracker is fully available in both English and Hindi. If you think in Hindi, or you're setting up the app for your parents, the whole experience can be in the language that feels natural.

That matters more than it sounds. A reminder in your own language lands differently from one you translate in your head. Small things like that make it easier to stick with a habit for months, not days.

It's also useful if you switch between languages through the day. Pick System and the app follows your phone's language, or change it in settings whenever you want.

## What works in Hindi

**The full interface.** Habit cards, the Today screen, reminders, Stats, the calendar, the weekly review and settings all appear in Hindi when you choose it.

**Voice logging.** Tap the mic and say what you did. The app understands Hindi number words, so “दो गिलास पानी” logs two glasses of water. It also converts units for you: minutes and hours, or metres, kilometres and miles.

It finds the matching habit and amount, then asks for one tap to confirm. It uses your phone's own speech recognizer, and the app never records audio. [Voice logging](/voice-logging) is part of Pro and the trial.

**The AI coach.** Ask “मेरी पढ़ाई की आदत टूट जाती है, क्या करूँ?” or the Hinglish “weekend pe routine bigad jata hai” and the coach replies in the same language. Chatting with the [AI coach](/ai-coach) is part of Pro and the trial; the free weekly insight is for everyone.

**Shortcuts.** Long-press the app icon for Voice log, Quick log and Start a run. Voice log is the quickest way to speak a habit in Hindi without opening the app first.

## Getting started in Hindi

1. Install the app and switch the language to Hindi.
2. Pick two or three habits from the [habit templates](/habit-templates), or create your own and name it the way you'd say it, like “सुबह की सैर” or “रात को फ़ोन दूर”.
3. Set a reminder for each one at a time that suits your day.
4. During the 7-day trial, try speaking your logs in Hindi to see if it suits you.

Start small. आज एक आदत, कल दूसरी (one habit today, another tomorrow) works better than ten habits on day one.
